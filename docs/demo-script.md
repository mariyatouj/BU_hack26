4. **1:40–2:15** Open Wallet. The 200-point opening credit and 100-point cooking award fund a simulated day pass. Confirm the reservation, read the simulation notice and show the debit and reference. Cancel once to restore stock and balance.
5. **2:15–2:45** Choose bus 1 in Explore and open Newbury Pantry. Point out the directional stop and return stop. In Access help, show the real GBFB locator, existing reduced-fare and cultural programs, and the attributed statewide statistic.
6. **2:45–3:00** Open Pantry editor and confirm Reset demo. Both tabs return to the starting records. Repeat with another route or recipe.

## Acceptance checklist

- A pantry-specific match shows mutually exclusive counts. Check canned versus dry forms, missing oil, a no-cook recipe and a recipe at two sites.
- Cuisine, equipment, diet, preparation time and home ingredients persist between search scopes. Surprise respects the active filters. Empty results offer a deliberate adjustment.
- A no-op stock edit leaves timestamps intact. An expired item, closure or stock-out changes current results. A saved pickup remains usable.
- Check inbound and return bus stops, Green branches and Red destinations. Test 5/10/15-minute walking limits. Try denying location, using the keyboard, selecting a neighborhood and disabling network access.
- Confirm that repeated photos, repeated records, fewer than three distinct foods and a fourth weekly award are rejected.
- Try an insufficient balance, competing reservations and a repeated cancellation. Refresh halfway through the flow, then reset. Photos remain private and can be deleted.
- Rehearse using both the normal production bundle and the single-file build.
